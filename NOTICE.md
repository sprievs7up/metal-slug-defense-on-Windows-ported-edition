# Notices

## English

This is an unofficial port of Metal Slug Defense. The original game, branding, artwork, and other game materials retain their existing ownership and notices. Upstream runtime components retain their respective licenses; the Windows package includes those notices in `windows_runtime/licenses/`. The generated native game code derives from the original Android game library. This repository does not assign a new blanket license to those materials.

The native Vorbis decoder includes stb_vorbis 1.22 under its MIT license. Its source and license are included in `src/third_party/`, and the Windows package includes `licenses/stb_vorbis.txt`.

The deterministic sin/cos used by netplay and replays (`src/netplay.cpp`) are ported from musl libc (MIT), which derives them from fdlibm (Sun Microsystems notice). Both notices are included in `licenses/fdlibm_musl.txt`.

The Plants vs. Zombies units (`community_content/pvz_*`) use materials from the Nintendo DS version of Plants vs. Zombies, taken from The Spriters Resource: https://www.spriters-resource.com/ds_dsi/pvszds/ . Those materials retain their existing ownership.

The MV-280C unit colours (`community_content/mv280c_out.obm`, `community_content/mv280c_bullet_out.obm`) are taken from the Metal Slug 6 MV-280C sprite sheet on The Spriters Resource, ripped by Division 6 (A Japanese Soldier). The sprite pixels come from the MV-280C VER.F atlas of the original game. Those materials retain their existing ownership.

The Giant Hunter Walker unit (`community_content/giant_hunter_walker.obm`) uses the Metal Slug 6 Giant Hunter Walker sprite sheet on The Spriters Resource, ripped by Division 6 (A Japanese Soldier). Its attack motion follows the Metal Slug Attack sprite sheets of the same unit on The Spriters Resource (ripped by Akapreston and Midian-P, arranged by Patchworks); those sheets were used only as a reference and are not included in the game. Its sound effects (`community_content/ghw_*.msdf`) are taken from the Metal Slug 6 stage 5 sound bank (`MS6_OST_ST05.osb`, converted with manatools); the spit sound was matched against Metal Slug Attack gameplay footage, and the egg impact sound, matched against Metal Slug Attack unit preview footage, is the original game's OgA_Burst. Those materials retain their existing ownership.

The Hunter unit (`community_content/hunter.obm`) uses the Metal Slug 6 Hunter sprite sheet on The Spriters Resource, ripped by The Mad Soldier; its dynamite explosion uses the original game's rebel infantry grenade explosion. Its voices for death, dynamite throws and machete attacks (`community_content/hunter_*.msdf`) are taken from the Metal Slug 6 stage 2 sound bank (`MS6_OST_ST02.osb`, converted with manatools). Those materials retain their existing ownership.

The Iron Cab unit (`community_content/iron_cab.obm`) uses the Metal Slug Attack Iron Cab sprite sheet on The Spriters Resource (ripped by Midian-P, arranged by Patchworks), scaled down to 0.65; its explosions and debris use the original game's shared effects; its unit icon in `community_content/unit_icon_02.obm` is the Iron Cab cell of the Metal Slug Attack 7.13.0 icon atlas `unit_icon_02.obme`. Its missile and explosion sounds (`community_content/ironcab_missile.msdf`, `ironcab_bomb.msdf`) are taken from the Metal Slug 6 stage 1 sound bank (`MS6_OST_ST01.osb`) and its laser sounds (`ironcab_charge.msdf`, `ironcab_laser.msdf`) from the stage 5 sound bank (`MS6_OST_ST05.osb`), converted with manatools. Those materials retain their existing ownership.

The Iron Sentinel unit (`community_content/iron_sentinel_0.obm`, `community_content/iron_sentinel_1.obm`, `community_content/iron_sentinel_descriptor.json` and the animation scripts in `community_content/units/iron_sentinel.json` and `iron_sentinel_form2.json`) is converted from the unit data of Metal Slug Attack 7.13.0 (SNK): the battle atlases `iron_slider_out.obm` and `ironiso_1_out.obm` and the action record of UnitID 287, with the parts drawn at full size instead of the original 75 % and the related offsets scaled accordingly; the front plate is placed against the hull and the roof plates as in the Metal Slug 6 sprite composite. Its unit icon in `community_content/unit_icon_02.obm` is the cell of this unit in the Metal Slug Attack icon atlas `unit_icon_02.obme`. Its walking and knockback sounds (`community_content/isentinel_walk.msdf`, `isentinel_knockback.msdf`) are the Metal Slug Attack files `cata2` and `knock_back_heavy`; its other sounds and explosions are the original game's shared sounds and effects. These materials retain their existing ownership.

Ura world 4 (Metal Slug 6 Mission 1) uses the Metal Slug 6 background sheets "Mission 1-1" and "Mission 1-2, 1-3 and 1-4" on The Spriters Resource, ripped by The Huntress: the battlefield images (`campaign_content/ms6_m1_*.webp`) are cut and combined from these sheets, and the stage thumbnails (`campaign_content/ms6_thumb_m1_*.png`) are reduced from the battlefields with the original game's STAGE lettering. Its background music (`community_content/bgm_ms6_m1_1.msdf`, `bgm_ms6_m1_2.msdf`, `bgm_ms6_m1_boss.msdf`) is decoded from the Metal Slug 6 ADX tracks `02_stg1_1`, `03_stg1_2` and `09_boss1`, keeping the original loop points. Its Thailand area (Metal Slug 6 Mission 2) likewise uses the sheets "Mission 2-1" and "Mission 2-2" by The Huntress: `campaign_content/ms6_m2_*.webp` are cut and combined from them (the fifth battlefield joins the watchtower-free stretches of the outpost strip), the thumbnails `campaign_content/ms6_thumb_m2_*.png` are made the same way, and `community_content/bgm_ms6_m2.msdf` is decoded from the ADX track `04_stg2`. Its China area (Metal Slug 6 Mission 3) uses the sheets "Mission 3-1", "Mission 3-2" and "Mission 3-3 and 3-4" by The Huntress: `campaign_content/ms6_m3_*.webp` are cut and combined from them (the bamboo pole of the second battlefield is extended with pixels of the same pole, and the road and front rubble of the fourth battlefield are joined from rubble-free stretches of the same street), the thumbnails `campaign_content/ms6_thumb_m3_*.png` are made the same way, and `community_content/bgm_ms6_m3.msdf` and `bgm_ms6_m3_boss.msdf` are decoded from the ADX tracks `05_stg3` and `10_boss2`, keeping the original loop points. Those materials retain their existing ownership.

The PVZ EVENT background music (`community_content/bgm_pvz_watery_graves.msdf`) is a Metal Slug style remix of "Watery Graves" from Plants vs. Zombies, published on Bilibili as "【NEOGEO×PVZ】后庭泳池-白天主题曲——《Watery Graves》（合金弹头风格Remix）". The composition and the remix retain their existing ownership.

The PVZ EVENT background music for the fifth area (`community_content/bgm_pvz_zombotany.msdf`) is a Metal Slug style remix of the unused Plants vs. Zombies track "Zombotany", published on Bilibili as "【NEOGEO×PVZ】废弃音乐《Zombotany》（合金弹头风格Remix）". The composition and the remix retain their existing ownership.

植物大战僵尸单位（`community_content/pvz_*`）使用 DS 版《植物大战僵尸》的素材，取材自 The Spriters Resource：https://www.spriters-resource.com/ds_dsi/pvszds/ 。这些素材保留原有归属。

MV-280C 单位（`community_content/mv280c_out.obm`、`community_content/mv280c_bullet_out.obm`）的配色取自 The Spriters Resource 上的《合金弹头6》MV-280C 素材表（Division 6 / A Japanese Soldier 整理），图像像素来自原游戏 MV-280C VER.F 的图集。这些素材保留原有归属。

巨型 Hunter Walker 单位（`community_content/giant_hunter_walker.obm`）使用 The Spriters Resource 上的《合金弹头6》Giant Hunter Walker 素材表（Division 6 / A Japanese Soldier 整理）；攻击动作参照同网站的《合金弹头 进攻》（Metal Slug Attack）同一单位素材表（Akapreston、Midian-P 提取，Patchworks 整理），该素材表只作参照，未放入游戏。其音效（`community_content/ghw_*.msdf`）取自《合金弹头6》第 5 关音效库（`MS6_OST_ST05.osb`，以 manatools 转出），吐卵音效按《合金弹头 进攻》的对战影片比对选定，卵落地音效按其单位动作浏览影片比对，为原游戏的 OgA_Burst。这些素材保留原有归属。

Hunter 单位（`community_content/hunter.obm`）使用 The Spriters Resource 上的《合金弹头6》Hunter 素材表（The Mad Soldier 整理），炸药的爆炸图像为原游戏叛军步兵的手榴弹爆炸。其死亡、投炸药与砍刀的语音（`community_content/hunter_*.msdf`）取自《合金弹头6》第 2 关音效库（`MS6_OST_ST02.osb`，以 manatools 转出）。这些素材保留原有归属。

IRON CAB 单位（`community_content/iron_cab.obm`）使用 The Spriters Resource 上的《合金弹头 进攻》（Metal Slug Attack）Iron Cab 素材表（Midian-P 提取，Patchworks 整理），缩小到 0.65 倍；爆炸与碎片为原游戏的共用效果；头像（`community_content/unit_icon_02.obm` 中）为 Metal Slug Attack 7.13.0 头像图集 `unit_icon_02.obme` 中 Iron Cab 的原格。其导弹与爆炸音效（`community_content/ironcab_missile.msdf`、`ironcab_bomb.msdf`）取自《合金弹头6》第 1 关音效库（`MS6_OST_ST01.osb`），激光音效（`ironcab_charge.msdf`、`ironcab_laser.msdf`）取自第 5 关音效库（`MS6_OST_ST05.osb`），以 manatools 转出。这些素材保留原有归属。

IRON SENTINEL 单位（`community_content/iron_sentinel_0.obm`、`community_content/iron_sentinel_1.obm`、`community_content/iron_sentinel_descriptor.json` 与 `community_content/units/iron_sentinel.json`、`iron_sentinel_form2.json` 中的动作脚本）由《合金弹头 进攻》（Metal Slug Attack）7.13.0（SNK）的单位数据转换：战斗图集 `iron_slider_out.obm`、`ironiso_1_out.obm` 与 UnitID 287 的动作记录，部件由原作的 75% 改为原尺寸绘制，相关偏移随之缩放；正面板与车身、顶盖的相对位置按《合金弹头6》的合成图调整。其头像（`community_content/unit_icon_02.obm` 中）为 Metal Slug Attack 头像图集 `unit_icon_02.obme` 中本单位的原格。其行走与受击音效（`community_content/isentinel_walk.msdf`、`isentinel_knockback.msdf`）为 Metal Slug Attack 的 `cata2` 与 `knock_back_heavy`；其余音效与爆炸为原游戏的共用音效与效果。这些素材保留原有归属。

里世界 4（《合金弹头6》任务 1）使用 The Spriters Resource 上的《合金弹头6》背景素材表“Mission 1-1”与“Mission 1-2, 1-3 and 1-4”（The Huntress 整理）：战场图像（`campaign_content/ms6_m1_*.webp`）由这两张素材表裁切拼合，关卡略缩图（`campaign_content/ms6_thumb_m1_*.png`）由战场缩小并写上原游戏的 STAGE 字样。其配乐（`community_content/bgm_ms6_m1_1.msdf`、`bgm_ms6_m1_2.msdf`、`bgm_ms6_m1_boss.msdf`）由《合金弹头6》的 ADX 曲目 `02_stg1_1`、`03_stg1_2`、`09_boss1` 解出，保留原版循环点。其「泰国」区域（《合金弹头6》任务 2）同样使用 The Huntress 整理的素材表“Mission 2-1”与“Mission 2-2”：战场图像（`campaign_content/ms6_m2_*.webp`）由这两张素材表裁切拼合（第 5 关由哨岗段中没有哨岗的几段拼成），关卡略缩图（`campaign_content/ms6_thumb_m2_*.png`）做法同上，配乐 `community_content/bgm_ms6_m2.msdf` 由 ADX 曲目 `04_stg2` 解出。其「中國」区域（《合金弹头6》任务 3）使用 The Huntress 整理的素材表“Mission 3-1”“Mission 3-2”与“Mission 3-3 and 3-4”：战场图像（`campaign_content/ms6_m3_*.webp`）由这三张素材表裁切拼合（第 2 关的竹竿以同一竹竿的像素延长，第 4 关的路面与路前碎石由同一街道中碎石少的几段拼成），关卡略缩图（`campaign_content/ms6_thumb_m3_*.png`）做法同上，配乐 `community_content/bgm_ms6_m3.msdf`、`bgm_ms6_m3_boss.msdf` 由 ADX 曲目 `05_stg3`、`10_boss2` 解出，保留原版循环点。这些素材保留原有归属。
